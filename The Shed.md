> This is a collection of tools, frameworks, and methods that I hope can be used to build and maintain [[Cognitive Architecture]]. Many will be perfectly suited to stand alone in your own work.

Moreover, this page is a place to store and organize these resources, making them easily accessible for future reference. Some of the tools and frameworks are my own, while others are from the community. I hope that this collection will be useful to you in your journey to build and maintain your own Cognitive Architecture.

*Check back often, as I will be adding new tools and frameworks as I find them - and also updating existing ones as I learn more about them. Maybe from someone like you!*
