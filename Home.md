# Welcome to Cognitive Architecture Collaborative
![[banner-w-borders.jpg]]

---
Hello. 

This site is first and foremost a [[digital garden]]. It may not look like much, but there's lots under the surface.

The notes and ideas you'll find here explore the intersection of knowledge, work, technology, philosophy, and culture. Some notes are for testing ideas, some for applying different lenses, and most are for figuring out how to keep humanity at the centre of increasingly complex technological landscapes.

 [[About the site|This won't always happen in a straight line]].

As much as this is a project of [[About me|one person]], it's also an invitation.
