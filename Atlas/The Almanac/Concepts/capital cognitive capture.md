> When your "style," decisions, and feedback become training data and managerial telemetry—extraction of behavioural data and cognitive patterns through interfaces mediating action.

## Definition
Capital Cognitive Capture is the extraction of behavioural data and cognitive patterns through the interfaces that mediate our work. It's a novel threat distinct from traditional automation concerns.

**The difference:**
- **Traditional automation** (Luddite framing): Machines replace jobs
- **Capital Cognitive Capture** (new threat): AI "data-fies" knowledge work / thought work itself; your patterns, decisions, style become training data.

## Why It Matters
This isn't simply AI replacing jobs; it's AI redefining what work means. In AI-mediated work, employability becomes "legibility-to-the-system plus access to proprietary context."

## Mechanisms
- **Extraction** — Behavioural patterns become training data
- **Telemetry** — System monitoring captures decision-making
- **Capture** — Intellectual property and style absorbed by platforms
