> [!tldr] Core Concept:
> Seven distinct role-based lenses for knowledge work, operationalizing the Cognitive Conservatory principle as embodied practice.

I initially got inspiration for this idea when I read the work of [Ward Cunningham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ward_Cunningham) and more specifically [this early-web archive](https://wiki.c2.com/) - I hope to expand the space in my [[Digital Gardens of the interwebs (a very narrow cross-section).|list of inspirations one day to talk more about this.]]

## Definition
---
Conservatory Roles decompose "knowledge stewardship" into specialized practices: **Gardener** (cultivation), **Cartographer** (mapping), **Refactorer** (optimization), **Archivist** (preservation), **Pollinator** (cross-linking), **Narrator** (synthesis/communication), and **Bridgekeeper** (boundary management).

## Key Claims
---
1. Roles structure epistemic work as distinct practices, not generic "knowledge work".
2. AI agents can mirror human roles to create coherent human-AI collaboration.
3. Role rotation prevents cognitive fatigue; role permanence enables depth.

## The Seven Roles
---

| Role             | Primary Function                       | Zone of Focus            |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| **Gardener**     | Cultivation, tending, growth tracking  | Values, habits, routines |
| **Cartographer** | Mapping, linking, sense-making         | Structure, connections   |
| **Refactorer**   | Optimization, simplification           | Code, prose, systems     |
| **Archivist**    | Preservation, provenance, logging      | Metadata, journals       |
| **Pollinator**   | Cross-linking, inspiration-seeding     | Ideas across domains     |
| **Narrator**     | Synthesis, communication, articulation | Publishing, speaking     |
| **Bridgekeeper** | Boundary design, context shifts        | Vault architecture       |

## Tensions / Open Questions
---
- Individual role adoption vs. role fatigue
- Structure vs. organic flow
- AI agent autonomy vs. human oversight in role-mirroring
- How to maintain role discipline without rigidity?

## Constraints
---
- Must preserve provenance tracking across all roles
- Must rotate roles intentionally (avoid "overstaying in one lens")
- Respect vault zone assignments per role
- "This isn't cosplay. It's structured reflection."
