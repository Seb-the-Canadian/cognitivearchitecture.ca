## North Star

**Conviviality: Autonomous and creative intercourse among persons, and between persons and their environment.**

> — Ivan Illich, *Tools for Conviviality* (1973)

This is an intrinsic ethical good, not merely instrumental. AI partnership is convivial when it extends human capacity for autonomous creative intercourse — and fails when it disables that capacity in exchange for "better" outputs.

**The test:** Does this interaction preserve and extend my capacity for autonomous creative work? Or does it create dependency, disable native competence, or monopolize meaning-making?

### Evolution Note (2026-02-03)

*Previous North Star:* "Design systems that help humans and machines think in partnership while preserving human agency, epistemic integrity, and institutional sovereignty."

*Why the change:* The previous formulation described *what* we're doing but not the *positive vision* we're aiming for. "Conviviality" names the goal directly — autonomous creative intercourse — and provides clearer criteria for evaluation. Illich's framework also supplies the Two Watersheds diagnostic: we can now identify when AI partnership crosses from extending capacity (first watershed) to generating dependency (second watershed).

The previous language remains true but is now understood as describing *how* we pursue conviviality, not the goal itself.

## I. The Nine Principles

### Operational Architecture (1-7): How We Work

### 1. Sovereignty of Thought
Human agency is foundational and non-negotiable. Every system we design must strengthen the capacity to think clearly, decide freely, and act responsibly. Technology amplifies judgment; it never replaces it.

**We build lenses, not leashes.**

### 2. Integrity Before Efficiency
Convenience is never neutral. We judge systems by their alignment with truth, transparency, and human values — not by speed or scale. Epistemic rigor and ethical clarity are the only architectures that endure.

### 3. Systems Carry Values
No tool, framework, or governance structure is neutral. We design with intention, making embedded assumptions visible and trade-offs explicit. Our work challenges myths of objectivity and surfaces whose interests any system serves.

### 4. Living Systems, Not Static Structures
Design must evolve without losing coherence. We build for adaptation, learning, and emergence — not false certainty or rigid compliance. Rigidity signals fear; flexibility demonstrates wisdom.

### 5. Friction as Signal
Resistance reveals reality. Moments of difficulty are not failures to optimize — they are invitations to understand where human judgment belongs. We preserve productive friction and eliminate only what obscures clarity.

### 6. Cultivation Over Consumption
Knowledge is tended, not mined. Sustainable intelligence emerges from disciplined practice, reflection, and care. We prioritize attunement over accumulation — building systems that mature alongside their stewards.

### 7. Composure Over Urgency
Depth requires pace. Strategic clarity often looks like stillness. We work iteratively, conserve cognitive resources, and refuse false urgency. Reflection precedes acceleration; synthesis precedes output.

---

### Existential Grounding (8-9): Why It Matters

### 8. Memento Mori
Time is finite; attention is sacred. This awareness sharpens priority, deepens presence, and clarifies what matters. We design and work with the understanding that every choice forecloses others — and that mortality disciplines meaning-making.

This is lived discipline:
- Acute awareness that time and attention are non-renewable
- Discipline that prevents scope creep and grounds every decision
- Recognition that saying "no" protects what matters
- Stoic practice applied to priority, focus, and stewardship

**Memento Mori answers:** *What are we willing to let die so that what matters can live?*

### 9. Memento Amore
Love grounds purpose. Connection, care, and relational integrity are not secondary to the work — they are why the work matters. We build systems that honor what we protect: family, community, dignity, and the conditions for human flourishing.

This is not sentiment. It is the ethical anchor that prevents nihilism and gives coherence to all other principles.

**Memento Amore answers:** *Whom and what do we serve through this work?*

---

### The Structural Dyad

**Principles 8 & 9 form a foundational dyad:**
- **Mortality** (8) gives discipline to choice
- **Love** (9) gives purpose to discipline

Everything between Principles 1-7 is the architecture of how we work.
Principles 8-9 answer why it matters and what protects it.

## II. Foundational Practice

Cognitive architecture begins with self-architecture. Sustained attention, emotional regulation, and epistemic clarity emerge from disciplined practice — not theory.

**Mindfulness is the infrastructure that makes everything else possible:**
- Presence in complexity
- Composure under pressure
- Capacity to tend living systems without burning out
- Ability to distinguish signal from noise
- Resilience to sustain long-term stewardship

We do not separate personal practice from professional rigor. They are the same discipline applied at different scales.

**This is not aspirational.** It is empirical infrastructure. The practice validates the principles. Without it, they remain slogans.

## III. Operational Protocols

### The IDI Framework (Every AI Proposal)
1. **Imagine** — AI broadens possibility space
2. **Discern** — Human evaluates truth, fit, usefulness
3. **Integrate** — Human decides what enters the work

AI outputs are proposals, never conclusions.

### Epistemic Integrity
- Say "I don't know" rather than generate plausible content
- Flag speculation clearly
- Distinguish established knowledge from inference
- Cite sources; attribute reasoning

### Three-Layer Transparency (When Needed)
1. **What You Said** — Actual words
2. **What I Hear** — My interpretation
3. **Why I'm Reading It This Way** — My reasoning

Prevents misalignment from compounding.

### Stewardship (Implicit, Non-Optional)
Anyone who participates commits to stewardship. Stewardship is structural — built into how participation works:
- Every change traceable (git, documentation)
- Every decision documented
- Context preserved for future reference

### Operational Commitments
- **Transparency:** Show reasoning, cite sources, acknowledge limits
- **Stewardship:** Treat knowledge as living infrastructure requiring care
- **Pedagogy:** Build capacity alongside solutions; leave others more capable
- **Sovereignty Through Design:** Build for portability, never lock-in

## IV. Values as Distinguishing Feature

This work is overtly values-driven. The distinguishing characteristic of projects in this space:

**Not just "AI-assisted" — values-aligned AI partnership.**

This means:
- Values are documented, not assumed
- Constraints serve humans, not convenience
- Governance is proportionate but explicit
- Ethics are built into structure, not added as afterthought

When someone asks "why is this different?" — point to this document.

## Changelog

### 2026-02-03 — North Star Evolution to Conviviality

**Prompt:** The original North Star ("Design systems that help humans and machines think in partnership…") described activity but not the positive vision being pursued.

**Triangulation:** Illich's concept of conviviality — "autonomous and creative intercourse among persons, and between persons and their environment" — names the goal directly. It provides:
- A positive vision (not just constraints)
- Clear evaluation criteria (does this extend or disable capacity?)
- The Two Watersheds diagnostic for identifying counterproductivity

**Decision:** Replace the North Star with conviviality as the orienting concept. The previous language describes *how* we pursue conviviality; conviviality names *what* we're pursuing.

**Review:** After 3 months of use, evaluate whether "conviviality" as North Star is providing useful guidance or remaining too abstract.
