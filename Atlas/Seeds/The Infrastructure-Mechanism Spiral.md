# The Infrastructure-Mechanism Spiral

**Theme:** Scale-agnostic infrastructure design

**Bridge:** [[social infrastructure]] ↔ Three Enabling Mechanisms ↔ Professional Knowledge Management (ProKM)

---

## Novel Connections

1. **[[social infrastructure]] ↔ [[Three Enabling Mechanisms]]**
   - Infrastructure provides the *context* where mechanisms operate
   - Mechanisms without infrastructure = unsupported interventions that fade
   - Infrastructure without mechanisms = inert systems that don't change behavior
   - *Neither works alone; they spiral together*

1. **[[Three Enabling Mechanisms]] ↔ [[Professional Knowledge Management]]**
   - ProKM instantiates all three mechanisms at personal scale:
     - **Language:** Relationship intelligence vocabulary
     - **Literacy:** Evidence-based capture discipline
     - **Loops:** Lattice enhancement through use
   - Personal mechanism practice seeds organizational adoption

3. **[[Professional Knowledge Management]] ↔ [[social infrastructure]]**
   - ProKM = personal social infrastructure for professional relationships
   - Same design question at different scales: "What systems enable collective flourishing?"
   - Personal infrastructure demonstrates possibility; organizational infrastructure scales it

---

## Throughline

**The Spiral Pattern:**
```
Personal Infrastructure (ProKM)
    ↓ demonstrates
Mechanism Effectiveness (Three Enabling)
    ↓ requires
Organizational Infrastructure (Social)
    ↓ enables
Personal Infrastructure at Scale
    ↓ recurses...
```

Infrastructure and mechanisms are not sequential but *recursive*. You can't build infrastructure without mechanisms to activate it, and you can't sustain mechanisms without infrastructure to support them. The spiral starts small (personal ProKM) and expands (organizational governance).
