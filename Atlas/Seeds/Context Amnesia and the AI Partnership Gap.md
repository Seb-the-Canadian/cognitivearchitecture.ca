# SEED-22: Context Amnesia and the AI Partnership Gap

**Theme:** AI limitations; why adoption doesn't produce ROI without relationship intelligence

**Bridge:** Context amnesia ↔ Knowledge fragmentation ↔ Professional knowledge management ↔ Bilingual fluency

---

## Working Title

Why Your AI Assistant Forgets Everything That Matters

## One-Sentence Thesis

AI assistants suffer from "context amnesia"—the inability to remember professional relationships and institutional dynamics—and this gap explains why most organizations see adoption without ROI.

## Reader Promise

Understand the specific mechanism limiting AI effectiveness in knowledge work, and what infrastructure would address it.

---

## Novel Connections

1. **[[Context Amnesia]] ↔ [[Knowledge Fragmentation Challenge]]** — Context amnesia is fragmentation at the AI layer: just as organizations suffer from scattered knowledge, AI assistants suffer from session-limited memory. The solutions mirror each other—both require explicit semantic architecture to bridge disconnected contexts. *Why it matters: connects individual AI limitation to organizational knowledge problem.*

2. **[[Professional Knowledge Management]] ↔ [[Dual-Mode Knowledge Model]]** — ProKM applies bilingual fluency principles to relationship intelligence: structured metadata (who, what, when) combined with natural language context (relationship dynamics, unwritten rules). Neither alone enables AI partnership; both together create persistent, usable context. *Why it matters: extends existing bilingual framework to novel domain.*
