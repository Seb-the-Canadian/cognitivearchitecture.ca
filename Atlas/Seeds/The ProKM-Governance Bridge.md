# SEED-28: The ProKM-Governance Bridge

**Theme:** Relationship intelligence as governance infrastructure; personal systems enabling organizational capability

**Bridge:** Professional knowledge management ↔ Three enabling mechanisms ↔ Shared governance language

---

## Working Title

Why Relationship Intelligence Is Governance Infrastructure

## One-Sentence Thesis

Professional Knowledge Management (relationship intelligence for AI) is the same infrastructure that makes governance culture operational—both require structured knowledge of who knows what and why it matters.

## Reader Promise

See how personal knowledge management systems become organizational governance enablers, and why investing in one builds the other.

---

## Novel Connections

1. **[[Professional Knowledge Management]] ↔ [[Framework - Three Enabling Mechanisms]]** — ProKM captures relationship intelligence (who, context, history); Three Enabling Mechanisms require exactly this for "literacy" and "feedback loops" to work. Governance mechanisms fail without relationship context—who to ask, who decides, who cares. ProKM is the substrate that makes governance mechanisms operational. *Why it matters: positions personal PKM as organizational governance infrastructure.*

2. **[[Professional Knowledge Management]] ↔ [[Shared Governance Language]]** — ProKM structures relationship knowledge; governance language structures decision knowledge. Both require translation from tacit to explicit. ProKM makes relationship context available to AI; shared language makes decision context available to humans. Together they create the bilingual infrastructure governance needs. *Why it matters: extends bilingual model to governance domain.*
