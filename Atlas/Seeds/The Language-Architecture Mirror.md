# SEED-30: The Language-Architecture Mirror

**Theme:** Language-system alignment; governance cynicism from expectation-experience gaps

**Bridge:** Shared governance language ↔ [[Default Decisions - Architecture Shapes Behaviour]] ↔ Technology as cultural enabler

---

## Working Title

When Governance Language Doesn't Match System Design

## One-Sentence Thesis

Shared governance language fails when it contradicts what systems actually do—the gap between "stewardship" talk and extractive architecture creates cynicism that destroys governance culture.

## Reader Promise

Understand why your governance language isn't landing, and what alignment between language and architecture requires.

---

## Novel Connections

1. **[[Shared Governance Language]] ↔ [[Default Decisions - Architecture Shapes Behaviour]]** — Language says one thing; architecture does another. "Your data, your control" means nothing if the system makes control hard. Language creates expectations; architecture creates experience. The gap creates cynicism—people stop trusting governance language entirely. *Why it matters: explains governance initiative failures despite good communication.*

2. **[[Shared Governance Language]] ↔ [[Technology Adoption & Change]]— Language is cultural; technology is cultural when designed intentionally. Misalignment means technology is not designed as cultural enabler—it's designed for efficiency while language is designed for trust. The mismatch surfaces immediately: employees experience the gap before members do. *Why it matters: positions employee experience as governance culture canary.*
