# SEED-27: Tool Congruence as Stewardship Test

**Theme:** Individual experience as organizational signal; tool fit predicts stewardship capacity

**Bridge:** [[Tool Congruence]] ↔ Member data stewardship ↔ Shared governance language

---

## Working Title

When Your Tools Don't Fit, Neither Does Your Mission

## One-Sentence Thesis

Tool congruence—systems aligned with thinking patterns—is the individual-scale version of member data stewardship, and organizations that ignore it at the personal level will fail to build it at the organizational level.

## Reader Promise

Understand why personal tool fit predicts organizational stewardship capacity, and what to do about misaligned systems.

---

## Novel Connections

1. **[[Tool Congruence]] ↔ [[Member Data Stewardship]]** — Tool congruence asks "does this system work with how I think?" Stewardship asks "does this organization work with how members need?" Same question at different scales. Organizations that force-fit tools on employees demonstrate they'll force-fit policies on members. *Why it matters: connects individual tool experience to organizational stewardship credibility.*

2. **[[Tool Congruence]] ↔ [[Shared Governance Language]]** — Tool congruence is achieved when system language matches thinking patterns. Shared governance language is achieved when policy language matches organizational culture. Both fail when external structures impose rather than align. Governance language that doesn't feel congruent won't be adopted. *Why it matters: explains why governance initiatives fail despite good intentions.*

---

## Core Throughline

- Personal tool fit is microcosm of organizational fit
- Organizations that impose tools will impose policies; both fail
- Congruence-first design is stewardship at every scale
