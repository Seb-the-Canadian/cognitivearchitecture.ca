# SEED-11: Only-Once Principle as Member Trust Infrastructure

**Theme:** Concrete operationalization of "your data, your control" through Estonia's e-governance model adapted to credit union context

**Bridge:** Member data stewardship (new CONCEPT-member-data-stewardship) ↔ Digital sovereignty (existing CONCEPT-digital-sovereignty), made operational through Only-Once Principle (new CONCEPT-only-once-principle)

[[How do we build civic social digital infrastructure alternatives?]]

---

## Novel Connections

1. **Estonia's e-governance model is operationalizable for cooperative banking.** "Citizens provide information once; government agencies reuse responsibly." This principle—adapted for members and credit unions—becomes concrete evidence that "your data, your control" is real, not marketing.

2. **Only-Once is governance culture made tangible.** It's not aspirational; it's measurable. "Member provides data X times per year" becomes a KPI. When it's 1, trust is operational. When it's 5, organization is extractive.

3. **Member experience changes when only-once is embedded.** Instead of "this credit union wastes my time asking the same thing repeatedly," members experience "this organization respects my time and treats me like I own my relationship." This is stewardship operationalized at the moment of interaction.

---

## First Paragraph Starter

> "Estonia solved a governance problem we still struggle with: citizens were providing information to government repeatedly, across different agencies. The solution—'only once'—means citizens provide information once; government agencies access it with transparent authorization. The principle is elegant and profound: respect citizen autonomy by not wasting their time. For credit unions, this becomes: members provide information once; organization reuses it responsibly with visible consent. This is not just efficiency; it's governance culture made visible. It's the moment a member experiences stewardship."

## Suggested Structure

1. **Open:** The repetition problem (Member experience)
   - Member applies for loan: provides address, income, family status
   - Member opens savings account: asked for address, income, family status again
   - Member updates mortgage: again
   - Member experience: "This organization doesn't know me; doesn't respect my time; treats me like I'm not trusted"

2. **Diagnosis:** Why repetition happens
   - System siloing: each department has separate data (no internal sharing)
   - Risk & compliance: each department verifies independently (don't trust other departments)
   - Organizational immaturity: no shared governance (no framework for responsible reuse)
   - Member impact: repetition + waste = erosion of trust

3. **Estonia Model:** How it works
   - Citizen provides information once to any government agency
   - Agency stores it responsibly (governance + encryption + audit trail)
   - Other agencies access with authorization + visible logging
   - Citizen sees: "Your tax data was accessed by pension office on [date]"
   - Citizen has control: can revoke access

4. **Credit Union Adaptation:** Making it operational
   - Member provides address, income documentation once
   - Stored with clear governance (who can access? Why? How long retained?)
   - Reused across products with visible consent
   - Member sees: "Your employment verification was used for mortgage pre-approval on [date]"
   - Member can revoke: "Don't reuse my income data for marketing"

5. **Implementation Sequence:**
   - Phase 1: Identify info reuse opportunities (what data is asked repeatedly?)
   - Phase 2: Build governance for reuse (who owns? Who can access? Audit trail?)
   - Phase 3: Implement consent workflows (member sees + approves)
   - Phase 4: Scale + measure (Only-Once KPI: target = 1 time/year for each info type)

6. **Competitive Advantage:** Why this matters
   - Member trust: "You actually respect me" vs. "You're mining me"
   - Operational efficiency: Reduced data capture/validation
   - Regulatory compliance: Cleaner audit trail + consent records
   - Differentiation: Large banks can't do this (requires stewardship culture + governance)

7. **Close:** Governance culture made visible
   - Only-Once principle is where stewardship meets member experience
   - Measurable: KPI (times member asked for same data)
   - Scalable: Works at any size organization (principle is the same)
   - Defensible: Built on stewardship + governance, not just efficiency
