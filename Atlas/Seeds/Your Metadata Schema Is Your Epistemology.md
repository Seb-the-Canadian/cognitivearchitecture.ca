# Your Metadata Schema Is Your Epistemology

**Theme:** Pedagogical tradecraft; metadata as cognition; architecture shapes behaviour at note level

## Working Title

Your Metadata Schema Is Your Epistemology

## One-Sentence Thesis

Pedagogical Tradecraft treats metadata not as organizational convenience but as encoded cognition—what you track, how you tag, which fields you require—these decisions shape how you think; architecture shapes behaviour at every scale, including the note.

## Reader Promise

Recognize that your tagging and frontmatter choices aren't just organizational preferences—they're shaping your thinking patterns.

## Tension / Counterpoint

- **Counterpoint:** Metadata is just organization; thinking happens in prose, not frontmatter
- **Your position:** Prose is where thinking is expressed. Schema is where thinking is *constrained*. What you're required to fill out is what you're required to consider. The schema doesn't replace thinking—it shapes which thinking you do. Unconstrained thinking sounds free; it's actually undisciplined.
