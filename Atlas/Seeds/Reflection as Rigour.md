## Working Title

Why Self-Regulation is a Leadership Skill, Not a Personal Practice

## One-Sentence Thesis

The skills that enable personal resilience—reflection, self-regulation, discernment—are the same skills that enable effective AI governance and technology leadership.

## Reader Promise

See how personal practices like reflection and Stoic self-regulation translate into organizational capabilities for technology governance.

---

## Novel Connections

1. **[[Philosophy of Enablement]] ↔ [[Concept - Intelligence vs Wisdom]]** — The enablement philosophy's "reflection as rigor" is wisdom in action: the capacity to pause, discern, and choose rather than react. This same capacity—cultivated through personal practices like DBT and Stoicism—is what distinguishes wise AI governance from reactive adoption. *Why it matters: connects personal development practices to organizational leadership capabilities.*

2. **[[Socratic Prototyping]] ↔ [[Framework - Hesitation-to-Learning Loop]]** — Socratic prototyping's "reflect & refine" phase institutionalizes the hesitation-to-learning loop: turning personal pause into organizational learning. Both require the same underlying capacity—patience with uncertainty, comfort with not-knowing, trust in emergence. *Why it matters: shows how individual reflection practices scale to organizational routines.*
