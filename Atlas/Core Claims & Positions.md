> [!map] About
> This map shows the current (and developing) claims and positions I use in my writing. 

While my intent is for [[About me|my]] writing here to reflect my honest growth & thought as it changes over time, where I'm starting from is grounded in some central positions. Some of these I've cleanly been able articulate for a little while now, others have revealed themselves to me as I've worked on these ideas, and some will inevitably emerge somewhere in the foggy future. I'm almost certain more than one will shift and take on a slightly new shape as I go along - in fact I really hope they will!

Below, I've organized these into loose categories and will reference them elsewhere if it seems worthwhile.

> [!command] ## Technology & Values
> ---
> #### **[[Technology should amplify human capacity for wisdom, connection, and flourishing.]]**
> *Not optimization for its own sake; but amplification of what makes us human.*
> 
> ---
> #### **[[Systems are not neutral; every technical choice shapes what can be expressed.]]**
> *Design encodes philosophy. Tool choice = values choice.*

> [!anchor] ## Knowledge Systems
> ---
> #### **[[Organizations fail when they build technical capability before semantic clarity.]]**
> *Shared meaning first, then systems.*
> 
> ---
> #### **[[Knowledge fragmentation is solved by semantic bridges, not just tool consolidation or acquisition.]]**
> *The problem isn't the tool, or tool sprawl; it's lack of connections.*
> 
> ---
> #### **[[Metadata enables discovery; natural language enables understanding; both required.]]**
> *Most systems optimize for one, lose the other. Need both.*
> [[Metadata]] should be ethical.
> 
> ---
> #### [[Knowledge is personal and political; there is no objective enablement.]]
> *All enablement choices embed values; teaching tools is never neutral.*

> [!user] ## Human Judgment & Wisdom
> ---
> #### **[[Data fluency is developmental, not binary; it cannot be mandated.]]**
> *You can't train fluency like you train literacy.*
> [[Those who are fluent operate on a culturally different level than those who are simply literate.]]
> 
>  ---
> #### [[Hesitation is often valuable signal, not resistance to overcome.]]
>  *Users pausing reveal what's missing. Listen before pushing.*
> 
>  ---
> #### [[The most powerful questions in (digital) transformation  are "should we" and "why", not "can we".]]
>  *Technical possibility ≠ good choice. Judgment matters.*
> 
> #### **[[Human wisdom cannot be replaced by (Artificial) intelligence.]]**
>  *Intelligence = processing. Wisdom = knowing whether to act.*

> [!industry] ## Governance & Power
> ---
> #### **[[In AI mediated work, employability becomes "legibility to the system plus proprietary context access"]]**
> *You're only valuable if you fit the system AND have its secrets. In a system that doesn't value your individual growth you're static & disposable.*
> 
> ---
> #### **[[Capital cognitive capture is more novel threat than traditional automation.]]**
> *Danger is "thought process extraction", not outright replacement.*
> 
> ---
> #### **[[Social opinion operates as enforcement mechanism of digital conformity.]]**
> *Big brother isn't (just) the "eye in the sky", it's also the opinion of everyone around us. The latter is more intrinsically motivating to social animals.*
> 
> ---
> #### **[[Default decisions shape platform behaviour; most users never change defaults.]]**
> *Sometimes defaults "work" because people don't change them.*
> See: [[Systems are not neutral; every technical choice shapes what can be expressed.]]; [[Default Decisions - Architecture Shapes Behaviour]]

> [!script] ## Knowledge Work Philosophy
> ---
> #### **[[Digital gardening is more sustainable than fast production.]]**
> *Slow work scales better. Authentic > polished.*

> [!globe] ## Equity & Perspective
> ---
> #### **[[Marginalized populations' experiences offer a crucial lens (on technology adoption).]]**
> *People at edges see what centre misses.*
