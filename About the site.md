> [!info] About
> This digital garden embodies its own principles through its implementation. Every technical choice, every architectural decision, every piece of documentation reflects the values and beliefs that shape the content itself. An emergent exploration of [[Cognitive Architecture|cognitive architecture]] itself.

> [!tower] The Perspective
> - Observations and field notes from the intersection of data strategy, AI enablement, and organizational systems thinking. 
> - Sustainable approaches to AI adoption while exploring how we can use technology to create more thoughtful, connected communities.
> - Rooted in an academic tradition studying how marginalized populations navigate institutional systems (from library policies affecting homeless patrons to youth homelessness services), how power is formed and wielded, and how data can be a tool for social good; shaping approaches to technology, culture, and organizational transformation.
> - Belief that technical solutions succeed or fail based on how well they integrate with how people actually work.
> 
> _For more about the person behind the work, [[About me|click here.]]_

> [!bike] The Approach
> - Sharing developing ideas & evolving claims rather than declaring solutions; working through frameworks and approaches, seeing what holds up when applied to real challenges.
> - Intentionally applying an "intersectional" or "non-traditional" set of lenses to both familiar and emergent problems.
> -  Purposeful and architected systems thinking over isolated solutions. Every technical choice has consequences that shape what can be expressed and how it can be understood.

> [!radar] What You'll Find
> -  [[The Grove|Essays exploring AI collaboration, digital sovereignty, and organizational transformation]] (polished thinking on [[Questions & Queries|questions I've been working through]]).
> - [[The Shed|Tools + Frameworks I'm using or developing for navigating technological adoption & working with AI.]]
> - **Knowledge systems** design and personal tooling approaches that seek to honour complexity and divergent perspectives.

> [!venetian] Intended Audience
> People who find themselves asking thoughtful questions about sustainable technology implementation & data sovereignty. If technology conversations feel like they move too fast or skip important human considerations, this space is designed with your perspective in mind.
